Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

AI Dungeon Master (the "App") is a product developed by Cytory. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

Authentication: We use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. We collect your Google ID and email address to manage your account and "Neural Pulse" balance.

Game Data: All game sessions, character statistics, and narrative logs are stored locally on your device or in your personal Google Drive (App Data folder). We do not store your campaign data on our servers.

2. Use of Information

We use your authentication data solely to:

  • Provide access to your account across multiple devices.
  • Manage and protect your "Neural Pulse" (in-app currency) transactions.
  • Provide technical support and essential service updates.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: For authentication and in-app purchases.
  • Google Drive API: To allow you to sync your game data to your own cloud storage. We only access the dedicated "App Data" folder and cannot see your other Google Drive files.
  • OpenAI / Anthropic: To generate AI narrative. Your actions are sent to these providers anonymously; no personally identifiable information (PII) is included in AI requests.

4. Data Retention

Account data (Google ID and pulse balance) is retained as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including cryptographically signed ID tokens for authentication, to protect your account and currency balance.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at support@neural-link.net.
