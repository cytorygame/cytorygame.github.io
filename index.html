Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Cytory free to play?

Yes! Cytory is completely free to download on Google Play. You can play campaigns using your daily free Neural Pulses, complete Operational Directives for free pulse rewards, or acquire optional Neural Pulses for extended story sessions.

How does the AI Game Master work?

Cytory utilizes advanced rules-aware LLMs trained on tabletop cyberpunk SRD mechanics. The GM calculates d20 skill checks, attribute modifiers, and equipment bonuses in real-time while generating adaptive narrative outcomes based on your actions.

Are my character sheets and campaign saves private?

Yes. Cytory enforces Local Data Sovereignty. All campaign logs, character sheets, and item inventories reside locally on your device or in your personal Google Drive (App Data folder). We do not store your narrative campaigns on our servers.

Can I play Cytory on Google Play Games for PC?

Yes! Cytory includes full x86_64 architecture support, mouse and physical keyboard navigation, and windowed/fullscreen scaling for desktop play on Google Play Games on PC.
