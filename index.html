Cytory features a sophisticated, rules-aware AI Game Master trained on comprehensive tabletop roleplaying frameworks and cybernetic rulesets. Every choice you make ripples across a living, breathing world with dynamic consequences, tactical combat, and reactive NPCs.

Choose your archetype—whether you're a disciplined Vanguard, a digital-weaving Cipher, a reconnaissance Pathfinder, an Enforcer, a MadTech, a Siren, or a Techie. Manage your core attributes, skill levels, and ability subroutines as your crew evolves.
