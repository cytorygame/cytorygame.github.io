PLAYER HANDBOOK

STARTER MANUAL & CORE OPERATIONAL PROTOCOLS

PHASE 01

Initialize Your Crew (Character Creation)

  • Navigate to Characters: Open the CHARACTERS tab from the bottom navigation bar and tap + NEW CHARACTER.
  • Select Origin & Archetype: Choose an origin (Human, Synthetic, Cyborg) to establish your base attributes, then select an archetype (Vanguard, Cipher, Techie, Enforcer, Pathfinder, MadTech, or Siren).
  • Allocate Core Attributes: Distribute stat points into POWER (Strength & Melee), REFLEX (Agility & Evasion), INTEGRITY (Hit Points & Armor), and NEURAL (Hacking & Technical Proficiency).
  • Portrait & Identity: Customize your unit's name, gender, and appearance using preset renders or custom neural generation.
PHASE 02

Mission Deployment (Launching a Campaign)

  • Navigate to Story: Tap the STORY tab and select + NEW CAMPAIGN.
  • Set Sector & Objective: Give your campaign an operational code name and choose a deployment sector (e.g., Cyber-Slums, Neon Heights, Corporate Core).
  • Assign Squad Leader: Choose your created character as squad lead.
  • Contract Briefing: Accept an active contract from the Mission Board or let the AI Game Master generate a narrative starting prompt.
PHASE 03

The Neural Terminal & Tactical Combat

  • Transmit Intent: Interact with the AI Game Master by typing custom text into the command line or using Quick Actions. Describe what your character attempts to do.
  • D20 Skill & Combat Checks: When an action carries risk or conflict, the AI GM requests a D20 dice check. Roll the digital dice directly on screen or enter physical tabletop dice results manually.
  • Tactical Rerolls: Spend 10 Neural Pulses to trigger a Tactical Reroll on critical checks or failed rolls.
  • Dynamic AI Narration: The AI GM tracks your active weapon, armor, and cyberware on every turn, weaving your specific loadout into combat descriptions and enemy responses.
PHASE 04

The Fleshsmith & Inventory Management

  • Interactive Item Inspection: Tap any item in your INVENTORY or the FLESHSMITH MARKETPLACE to open its Item Detail card. This displays full Lore Data, Functional Specs, Damage/AC Stats, and Item Properties before equipping or purchasing.
  • Fleshsmith Marketplace: Earn Credits (CR) by completing contracts and missions. Visit the Fleshsmith in the MARKET tab to acquire new weapons, ballistic armor, and cybernetic implants.
  • Equipping Gear: Open the INVENTORY screen to view active loadout slots:
    • WEAPON Slot: Sets active damage dice (e.g., 1d8 slashing, 2d8 heavy) and attack bonuses.
    • ARMOR Slot: Instantly increases your Armor Class (AC), reducing incoming damage and enemy hit chance.
    • EQUIPMENT CHASSIS & UTILITY BELT: Carry up to 3 active equipment pieces and 4 combat items (stim-packs, med-kits).
    • TRANSPORT BAY: Store active cyber-vehicles for mobility and rapid deployment.
  • System Strain & Cyberware: Installing cybernetic implants requires System Strain. Increase your INTEGRITY attribute to expand your Max Strain capacity and prevent cyberpsychosis.
PHASE 05

Neural Tuning & Interactive Character Sheets

  • Interactive Stat Tooltips: On your CHARACTER SHEET or PLAYER CARD, tap any core attribute (Power, Reflex, Integrity, Neural) or skill to display instant explanatory tooltips detailing how that stat influences combat, evasion, and skill checks.
  • Earning Upgrade Points (PTS): Advance your character by earning PTS from successful missions and narrative achievements.
  • Neural Tuning Facility: Open character details and tap NEURAL TUNING to spend PTS:
    • Attribute Upgrades: Permanently raise POWER, REFLEX, INTEGRITY, or NEURAL scores to boost dice roll modifiers and Max HP.
    • Ability Subroutines: Upgrade class-specific active abilities and reduce cooldown timers.
  • Stat Modifiers in Combat: Higher attributes increase your roll modifiers (e.g., a Reflex score of 14 grants a +2 modifier to attack rolls and evasion checks).
PHASE 06

UI HUD & Menu Directory (Button-By-Button Guide)

  • MAIN SCREEN BUTTONS & TABS:
    • STORY Tab: View active campaigns, start a new story, relocate sectors, or archive completed missions.
    • CHARACTERS Tab: Access the Character Lab to create new units, edit identities, or manage character sheets.
    • MARKET Tab: Open the Fleshsmith Marketplace to buy weapons, armor, cyberware, and transport vehicles.
    • ₱ULSES Tab: Collect daily pulse rewards, watch optional rewarded transmissions, or purchase pulse recharges.
    • TOP 3-DOT MENU (⋮): Access SETTINGS / OPTIONS, CLOUD BACKUPS, SYSTEM MANUAL, TRANSMIT FEEDBACK, and ABOUT / DISCORD.
  • IN-STORY HUD & TERMINAL BUTTONS:
    • CHARACTER PORTRAIT / CARD: Tap your squad leader's top-left portrait to inspect live HP, Energy, System Strain, AC, and attribute stats.
    • INVENTORY Button: Opens the live loadout overlay to equip weapons, equip armor, or use combat stims during a story session.
    • QUICK ACTIONS: Tap pre-generated contextual action buttons (e.g., Look Around, Assess Threat, Hack Terminal, Rest) for fast 1-tap responses.
    • ABILITIES Bar: Trigger class-specific active subroutines (Titan Slam, Tactical Scan, Blade Vortex, Shield Overload).
    • COMBAT HUD: Displays active initiative turn order, enemy HP status, and combat log during tactical encounters.
    • PAUSE / MENU: Return to the Main Menu or open settings while preserving current campaign state.
PHASE 07

Rest & Recovery Protocol

  • Resting: Take a Short or Long Rest in the terminal to restore Hit Points (HP) and Energy.
  • Sector Ambush Risk: Resting in hostile or unsecure sectors carries a risk of enemy ambushes! Clear immediate threats or secure a safehouse before initiating a rest sequence.
PHASE 08

Daily Directives & Pulse Management

  • Daily Directives: Check the Mission Board daily to complete objectives and earn free Credits & Neural Pulses.
  • Pulse Recharges: Transmitting an action message costs 1 Neural Pulse. Earn additional pulses through daily rewards, watching optional rewarded transmissions, or purchasing pulse packs in the Store.
PHASE 09

Saves, Cloud Backup & Story Export

  • Local Sovereignty: All session logs and character templates save automatically to your device's local storage for fast, offline-capable play.
  • Cold Storage Archiving: Archive inactive campaigns to Cold Storage to keep your active story list clean without losing progress.
  • Google Drive Cloud Sync: Go to the OPTIONS screen anytime to back up campaign snapshots to 1 of 5 Google Drive slots or restore save files across devices.
  • Google Docs Story Archive: Compile full campaign narration, choices, and combat logs directly into an editable Google Doc in your Google Drive.

⚠️ KNOWN ISSUES & TROUBLESHOOTING: NEURAL DESYNC & OVERLOAD

  • Network & AI Model Saturation: Cytory relies on real-time neural LLM generation. During periods of high global traffic or temporary internet connection drops, you may encounter a NEURAL_DESYNC or NEURAL_OVERLOAD alert.
  • What to Do During a Desync:
    • If a roll check or action fails to respond, tap RETRY on the alert pop-up.
    • Wait 5–10 seconds for the neural link to stabilize before resending your command.
    • Your character stats, items, and campaign progress are protected locally and will not be lost during a connection interruption.

⚡ CORE OPERATIONAL RULES TO REMEMBER

  • Narrative Authority: The AI Game Master is the arbiter of reality. State what your unit attempts to do—the GM determines success, consequences, and environmental reactions based on your stats and gear.
  • Tabletop Physical Dice Support: When a d20 roll is required, you can use the animated digital die or roll physical tabletop dice and input your physical roll result.
  • Local Sovereignty: You own your data. Character sheets and session logs reside on your local storage unless manually uploaded to cloud backup slots.
