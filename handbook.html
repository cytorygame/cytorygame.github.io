Origins & Archetypes (Class Specifications)

Every unit initialized in Cytory begins with a baseline Origin and Class Archetype that dictates stat scaling, abilities, and combat hit dice:

Base Origins

  • Human: Balanced biological entity with adaptable baseline attribute scaling.
  • Augmented Human: Cybernetically modified baseline with elevated Reflexes and Integrity.
  • Synthetic: Fully artificial android shell with high Neural and System Strain capacity.
  • Cyborg: Heavy machine-flesh hybrid engineered for armor absorption and frontline combat.
  • Netborn: Digital consciousness encased in a light chassis optimized for neural hacking.

SECTION 02

Dynamic Narrative Waves & One-Shot Arc Pacing

Every story campaign in Cytory flows through structured, 75-turn One-Shot Narrative Waves governed by real-time neural pacing. Starting a new mission or concluding an old one resets the wave counter to Turn 1 for that specific objective:

The 5-Phase One-Shot Narrative Arc

Phase Turns Cumulative Time Equivalent Narrative Objective
1. The Hook 5 5 Turns 25 Mins Immediate action; drop straight in media res with high sensory tension.
2. Introduction 10 15 Turns 50 Mins Establish main objective, initial scouting, roleplay, fixer briefings.
3. Complication 20 35 Turns 1 Hr 40 Mins Social puzzles, environmental hazards, or mini-combat skirmishes; clue gathering.
4. The Climax 30 65 Turns 2 Hrs 30 Mins Multi-phase boss battle, high-stakes confrontation, or complex dramatic resolution.
5. Conclusion 10 75 Turns 50 Mins Fallout, debriefing, contract credit payouts, campaign seeds.
TOTAL ARC 75 75 Turns 6 Hrs 15 Mins Complete One-Shot Adventure Arc

Dynamic Phase Acceleration & Continuous Waves

  • Mission-Relative Turn 1: Accepting a new contract resets the pacing clock to Turn 1 for that objective, ensuring every mission gets a complete 5-phase story arc.
  • Dynamic Acceleration: Critical successes (Natural 20s) or tactical brilliance can accelerate phases, skipping complications straight to the Climax!
  • Exfiltration & Escape: Aborting, escaping, or failing a mission jumps directly to Phase 5 (Conclusion) with appropriate fallout and exfiltration consequences.
  • Continuous Narrative Waves: If you never accept a formal mission board contract, the AI GM generates continuous 75-turn narrative waves. Upon reaching Turn 75, the GM concludes the current story arc and seamlessly launches into the next Narrative Wave (The Hook - Turn 1)!
SECTION 03

Core Attributes & D20 Roll Math

Every mechanical check in Cytory follows the classic D20 tabletop resolution formula:

FINAL TOTAL = d20 ROLL + ATTRIBUTE MODIFIER + PROFICIENCY BONUS + GEAR BONUSES

Core Attribute Breakdown

  • POWER (POW): Physical strength, melee weapon damage, forcing heavy doors, athletic maneuvers.
  • REFLEX (RFL): Agility, ranged precision, initiative order, and Armor Class ($\text{AC} = 10 + \lfloor\text{Reflex} / 2\rfloor + \text{Armor}$).
  • INTEGRITY (INTG): Vitality & System Strain. Calculates $\text{Max HP} = 10 + \lfloor\text{Integrity} / 2\rfloor \times 5$ and $\text{Max Strain} = \lfloor(10 + \text{Integrity}) / 2\rfloor + 2$.
  • NEURAL (NEUR): Cyberdeck speed, terminal hacking, decrypting data drives, executing digital subroutines.
  • INSIGHT (INS): Perception, hazard detection, searching rooms for hidden loot, reading tactical ambushes.
  • INFLUENCE (INF): Bribing, coercing, deceiving, and negotiating with corporate executives or fixers.

Modifier Formula

Your modifier is calculated directly from your attribute score:

Attribute Modifier = (Attribute Score - 10) / 2 [Rounded Down]

  • Natural 20 (Critical Success): Triggers maximum narrative outcome, accelerates story phases, and doubles damage dice in combat!
  • Natural 1 (Critical Failure): Triggers weapon jams, tactical setbacks, or environmental complications.
  • Tabletop Physical Dice Mode: When the d20 roll modal appears, you can use the animated digital die or roll physical tabletop dice and type in your exact physical result.
SECTION 04

The Fleshsmith, Equipment & Vehicles

  • Interactive Item Inspection: Tap any item in your INVENTORY or the FLESHSMITH MARKETPLACE to open its Item Detail card. This displays full Lore Data, Functional Specs, Damage/AC Stats, and Item Properties before equipping or purchasing.
  • Loadout Slots:
    • WEAPON Slot: Equipping weapons (*Mono-Blade*, *Plasma Pistol*, *Heavy Railgun*) updates active damage dice (1d8, 2d8) and attack bonuses.
    • ARMOR Slot: Equipping ballistic armor (*Ballistic Vest*, *Subdermal Plating*) directly boosts Armor Class (AC), reducing enemy hit chance.
    • EQUIPMENT CHASSIS & UTILITY BELT: Carry up to 3 equipment chassis pieces and 4 utility belt combat consumables (*Stim-Packs*, *Medkits*).
    • TRANSPORT BAY (Vehicles): Store active cyber-vehicles (*Cyber-Bike*, *AV-4 Transport*) for sector transit, fast travel, and road combat encounters.
  • System Strain & Cyberpsychosis: Cybernetic implants require System Strain. Raising your INTEGRITY expands your strain limit to prevent overload.
SECTION 05

Neural Tuning & Interactive Character Sheets

  • Interactive Stat Tooltips: On your CHARACTER SHEET or PLAYER CARD, tap any core attribute (Power, Reflex, Integrity, Neural) or skill to display instant explanatory tooltips detailing how that stat influences combat, evasion, and skill checks.
  • Earning Upgrade Points (PTS): Complete contracts and major story milestones to earn PTS.
  • Neural Tuning Facility: Tap NEURAL TUNING on your character details screen to spend PTS:
    • Attribute Upgrades: Permanently raise POWER, REFLEX, INTEGRITY, or NEURAL scores to boost dice roll modifiers and Max HP.
    • Ability Subroutines: Level up class-specific abilities to increase damage, range, and reduce cooldown timers.

⚠️ KNOWN ISSUES & TROUBLESHOOTING: NEURAL DESYNC & OVERLOAD

  • Network & AI Model Saturation: Cytory relies on real-time neural LLM generation. During periods of high global traffic or temporary internet connection drops, you may encounter a NEURAL_DESYNC or NEURAL_OVERLOAD alert.
  • What to Do During a Desync:
    • If a roll check or action fails to respond, tap RETRY on the alert pop-up.
    • Wait 5–10 seconds for the neural link to stabilize before resending your command.
    • Your character stats, items, and campaign progress are protected locally and will not be lost during a connection interruption.
